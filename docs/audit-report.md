# Audit report: original prototype

**Subject:** `original_prototype/index.html` (27 KB, one file, brand "VERDANT"), preserved unchanged and served at `/original/index.html`.
**Method:** a real browser run (Playwright, Chromium 1440×900) driven by `scripts/audit-original.mjs`. It took screenshots of all four stages, a rotated view, a container resize and a mobile layout (`docs/audit/original-*.png`), plus runtime probes for the keyboard, the mouse wheel, the animation loop and resizing. After that I reviewed the source.

**Main finding.** The prototype is not a 3D renderer. It is a 2D `<canvas>` that projects polygons by hand and sorts them back to front by their *average* depth before drawing (the painter's algorithm). That sorting technique causes the most serious defects (#1–#3), and they cannot be fixed reliably without splitting polygons. The scene was therefore migrated to WebGL (three.js via React Three Fiber), where the GPU's depth buffer resolves visibility per pixel. Every defect below is fixed in the new implementation and none was patched in the original. Where it was practical, a regression test now guards each fix.

No JavaScript exceptions occurred. All defects are functional, visual or integration problems.

| # | Issue | Severity | Root cause | Fix | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In stages 2–4 the house covers 5 of the 6 beds. Plant markers float over empty lawn (`original-stage-2.png`) | Critical | Painter's algorithm: the large house faces sort after the small bed faces | WebGL depth buffer. Layout data guarantees no overlap | `geometry.test.ts` (zones vs house, terrace, hedges); visual baselines |
| 2 | Rotating the camera makes the house disappear or the scene fall apart (`original-rotated.png`) | Critical | Same sorting problem, plus an unclamped elevation projection | Real perspective camera; OrbitControls polar limits (0.28–1.22 rad) and distance limits | E2E "dragging rotates the view…" |
| 3 | Irrigation pipes are drawn across the roof (`original-stage-4.png`) | High | Sorting, and pipe routes taken straight through the house footprint | Pipes routed through aisle lanes; drawn with depth testing | `geometry.test.ts` "keeps ground pipes outside the beds and the house", "never crosses two laterals" (mutation-checked) |
| 4 | The canvas stretches when its container resizes, e.g. inside a slide (`original-container-resize.png`). The backing store stayed at 1044 px while the element was 498 px | High | `resize()` listened only to `window.resize` | R3F measures its container with a ResizeObserver. The garden centres itself beside the side panel through a projection view offset | E2E "the canvas follows container resizes"; presentation "garden resizes with the slide frame and in full screen" |
| 5 | Arrow keys change stages from anywhere on the page. The probe pressed → while another control had focus and the stage changed | High (blocks embedding) | Global `keydown` listener with no checks on focus or `defaultPrevented` | Prototype key handling is off in presentation mode, and the deck owns the arrow keys (`deckKeyAction`) | `keyboard.test.ts`; `GardenScene.test.tsx` "never in presentation mode" |
| 6 | The mouse wheel is always captured (`defaultPrevented: true`) | Medium | Wheel handler on the canvas with no scoping | OrbitControls handles the wheel only over the canvas. The deck never navigates on wheel | Integration test "does not navigate on … wheel" |
| 7 | "Why this recommendation?" opens a blocking `window.alert` | Medium | Quick placeholder | An in-panel decision card with a plain-language explanation (`IrrigationDecision`) | `overlays.test.tsx`; E2E checks the explanation text |
| 8 | Plant health shows as a precise percentage ("87 %") in stage 2, before any sensor exists, and is implied to be measured | Medium (claim) | Data model mixed estimates with measurements | The health value is computed by a model, is always labelled "Estimated", drawn as a dashed line, and comes with a method note. It is never shown as measured. Only soil probes and the weather station measure anything | `sensors.test.ts` "marks plant health as estimated…"; `overlays.test.tsx` |
| 9 | Six moisture readings but only three sensor posts | Medium (claim) | Inconsistent fixture | One soil probe per bed plus a weather station; each reading belongs to a device | `sensors.test.ts` "exactly one soil probe per raised bed" |
| 10 | Irrigation decisions are not computed. Each species carries a hard-coded action and volume (`action: 'Water this zone'`, `liters: '1.8 L'`), so changing a reading or the weather cannot change a decision, and "scheduled vs adaptive" cannot be shown | Medium | Decision logic and text lived in the data and rendering code | A pure `decideAdaptive` / `decideScheduled` / `planIrrigation`, with input validation, a weather scenario and litres derived from root-zone volume | `irrigation.test.ts` (includes the brief's tomato example and invalid inputs) |
| 11 | Moisture labels overlap each other and the markers. Labels use colour alone | Medium | Fixed pixel offsets, no layout | Compact chips in the DOM, anchored in 3D, with a status dot *and* text, only on the active stage. The irrigation chips were shortened after a manual screenshot review found overlaps at slide size | Visual baselines `prototype-*.png`, `presentation-garden-irrigation.png` |
| 12 | The `requestAnimationFrame` loop runs forever (54 callbacks/s while idle) and redraws every frame in stage 4 | Low–Medium (performance, battery) | Unconditional `frame()` loop | `frameloop="demand"`: frames are requested only while something moves. On hidden slides `frameloop="never"` | E2E checks `data-settled` toggles; integration test checks `active=false` when hidden |
| 13 | Planned beds are almost invisible in stage 1 (white on white); only one is readable | Low | Bed colour equal to the ground colour | Dashed architectural outlines with a selectable fill and hover/selection states | Visual `prototype-planning.png`; E2E clicks a zone in 3D |
| 14 | Clicks only work on the number markers, not on plants | Low | Hit-testing against six screen points | Raycasting on real meshes (instanced plants, beds, enlarged invisible hit volumes for devices). A drag is never treated as a click (`e.delta > 5`) | E2E clicks tomato, pear tree, probe and zone through `clickInScene` |
| 15 | Canvas text "SITE MODEL / NOT TO SCALE" is distorted after a resize, and the claim contradicts the plan view | Low | Text drawn into the scaled backing store | Removed. The planning stage shows real dimension lines (8.00 / 7.00 / 28.00 m …) | Visual `prototype-planning.png` |
| 16 | Branding mismatch: the prototype says "VERDANT", the deck says "PlantWiz" | Low | Separate origins | Rebranded to PlantWiz using the PPTX palette and typefaces | Visual baselines |

Not reproducible / not applicable: hydration problems (the original was not React) and broken imports or missing assets (the file is self-contained).
